Company Medical Benefits Policy

Eligible employees receive medical insurance coverage from their date of joining, subject to the terms of the insurance provider.

Coverage
- Individual annual coverage limit: INR 5,00,000.
- Family floater annual coverage limit: INR 10,00,000.
- Outpatient consultations and prescribed medicines: covered up to INR 25,000 per policy year.
- Inpatient hospitalization, surgery, and emergency treatment: covered within the annual coverage limit.
- Maternity benefits: INR 75,000 for a normal delivery and INR 1,00,000 for a cesarean delivery.
- Preventive health checkups: up to INR 5,000 per policy year.
- Coverage for eligible dependents, subject to policy limits.

Claims
Employees should obtain pre-authorization for planned hospitalization when required. For reimbursement claims, submit original bills, prescriptions, discharge summaries, and claim forms to the HR or Benefits team within 30 days of the expense.

Exclusions
Pre-existing conditions, cosmetic procedures, and self-inflicted injuries are subject to the insurance provider's terms and may not be covered.

For policy documents, coverage limits, or claim questions, contact the HR Benefits team.
