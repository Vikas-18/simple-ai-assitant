Provident Fund Policy

The company operates a provident fund for employees in accordance with applicable law and plan rules.

Contributions
The provident fund is calculated on Basic Salary plus Dearness Allowance (DA).
- Employee contribution: 12% of Basic Salary plus DA.
- Employer contribution: 12% of Basic Salary plus DA.
- The statutory PF wage ceiling is INR 15,000 per month unless the employee has an applicable higher-salary arrangement.
- Example: If Basic Salary plus DA is INR 30,000, the statutory monthly employee PF deduction is 12% of INR 15,000 = INR 1,800.
- Example: If Basic Salary plus DA is INR 12,000, the employee PF deduction is 12% x INR 12,000 = INR 1,440.
- At the standard statutory ceiling, the employer contributes INR 1,800 per month. The employer's contribution may be split between EPF and EPS according to prevailing statutory rules.

PF Deduction Formula
Monthly Employee PF = 12% x Minimum(Basic Salary + DA, INR 15,000)

Vesting
Employee contributions belong to the employee according to plan terms. Employer contributions may vest over time according to the approved vesting schedule.

Withdrawals
Early withdrawals may be permitted for eligible circumstances such as retirement, resignation, medical treatment, education, housing, or other qualifying needs. Required approvals and waiting periods apply.

Nomination
Employees should register nominees and update them after major life events. Nomination status may determine how the fund balance is distributed.

Statements and Support
Employees can view their fund balance and contribution history through the designated fund portal or payroll channel. HR can assist with enrollment, tax, nomination, and withdrawal questions.
