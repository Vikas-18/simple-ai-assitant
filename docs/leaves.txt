Company Leave Policy

Employees should submit leave requests in the leave management system and provide advance notice wherever possible.

Leave Types
- Annual leave: 18 days per calendar year for full-time employees. Employees with fewer than 12 months of service receive 1.5 days per month worked.
- Sick leave: 12 days per calendar year. Unused sick leave expires at the end of the year and is not carried forward.
- Casual leave: 7 days per calendar year. Unused casual leave expires at the end of the year.
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks for an eligible employee giving birth, subject to applicable law.
- Paternity leave: 15 calendar days following the birth or adoption of a child.
- Bereavement leave: 5 working days for the death of an immediate family member.
- Compensatory leave: 1 day for every full approved holiday shift or 8-hour approved extra-duty period.

Leave Balances
- Maximum annual and casual leave carryover: 30 combined days.
- Leave balances are reset on January 1.
- Advance approval is required for 3 or more consecutive leave days.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days.

Approval
The employee's manager reviews leave requests based on workload and staffing. Leave is officially confirmed only after approval.

Unplanned Absence
Employees must notify their manager and the HR team as soon as reasonably possible. Leave records must be updated when the employee returns.

Carryover
Unused leave may be carried over or encashed only according to the applicable leave plan and local law.
