Company ESOP Policy

An Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP) allows eligible employees to receive or purchase company shares under an approved plan.

Eligibility
Eligibility is determined by role, joining date, employment status, and plan rules. Not every employee is eligible for a grant.

Grants
An ESOP grant specifies the number of options or units awarded, the grant date, exercise price if applicable, vesting schedule, and expiration date.

Vesting
Standard grants use a 4-year vesting schedule: 25% of options vest after 12 months of service, followed by monthly vesting of 1/48 of the grant for each additional month.
Example: A grant of 4,800 options will vest 1,200 options after the first 12 months and 100 options per month thereafter.

Exercise
Eligible vested units may be exercised through the designated equity portal. Exercise may require payment and can have tax implications.

Termination
Vesting generally stops when employment ends, subject to the grant agreement and plan terms. Employees must act within the post-termination exercise window stated in the grant documents.

Important
ESOP value depends on company performance and market conditions. Grants do not guarantee a financial return. Employees should review plan documents and seek independent financial or tax advice where needed.
